## The reading

Four moves, one process:

1. **THE COARSE LINE FIRST.** Given A and B, find the simplest effective
   route at the top altitude — a skeleton, not a finished plan.
2. **THE HOLON CHAIN.** Each leg of the line breaks into its necessary
   parts, those break again, down until legs are atomic (buildable /
   executable / lived). Planning and building are the same descent.
3. **THE FRONTIER CONSTRAINT.** Candidate steps come only from ideas
   connected to A's side, B's side, or something already touched —
   bidirectional, spreading through HIS net, never the full answer
   space. The net is the heuristic. (This is the exact disease that
   killed classic planners — combinatorial explosion — cured by making
   relevance structural.)
4. **RANDOM SEEDS, FPTP-JUDGED.** A few random candidates injected to
   escape connectivity deserts; each cheaply accepted or rejected
   (the 50-cheap-ops doctrine, already proven as the interpreter).

**Generation is the same process.** A story sentence = plan from
opening state to the thing you are trying to say: skeleton parts first,
target end held fixed, then the level of fill that reaches the end
fluently. Building a sentence, building a plan, deriving a future
state — one machinery.

## The candidate unifying rule (assistant proposal, to be courted)

**Refine until every adjacent pair is a lived connection.**

- A plan leg is atomic when its transition is an act he can run or a
  whole he has lived — stop descending there.
- A sentence is fluent when every adjacent unit-pair is a lived door
  (or a lived frame transition) — "level of fill" = the MINIMAL fill
  that makes every transition legal. Fluency = local closure.
- A future-state derivation is certified when every link in the chain
  is a held law — the same closure test, aimed forward.

One stop rule for plans, sentences, and forecasts. If it courts true,
the three lanes share one mechanism; if it fails somewhere, the
failure shape tells us which lane needs its own rule.

## THE BRIDGE LOOP (owner refinement, same session, verbatim)

> "now to reiterate, it can loop, but it would need a bridge from a to
> b. so all connectsions from a and all from b. see which ones have the
> closest approximal overlap with the goal state. select those and then
> pull the connections from those. then pull the connections from
> those, etc. you can use any of a b c or however many states, but it
> is preferred to use the answer space that satisfies the most
> conditions, as well as latent conditions like tools, availability,
> means, resources, time, money, etc."

The algorithm, named: **bidirectional beam search, overlap-scored.**

1. RING OUT: take all connections from A and all from B (both
   frontiers at once).
2. SCORE: each candidate graded by closest approximate overlap with
   the goal state — and this function ALREADY EXISTS as the oldest
   primitive in the codebase: compare() (shared / only_a / only_b /
   similarity-as-percentage). The beam heuristic is the single
   cognitive primitive, aimed at the goal.
3. SELECT: keep the best-overlap few (the beam), discard the rest —
   FPTP economics at every ring.
4. LOOP: pull the connections of the kept, score, select, again —
   until the two frontiers TOUCH (the bridge) or the budget is spent
   (honest refusal + the missing-leg ask).

**N states, not two:** A, B, C, however many — the preferred answer
space is the one that satisfies the MOST conditions simultaneously.
Scoring generalizes: candidate's doors vs the UNION of all states'
condition-sets; count satisfied. Multi-constraint satisfaction as
overlap counting — still compare().

**Latent conditions** (tools, availability, means, resources, time,
money) ride as preconditions on act: nodes — what an act NEEDS, not
just what it reaches. Research question filed: latent conditions must
be EARNED, not installed — the natural teacher is the failure node
(a plan that runs and dies because a tool was absent banks that
precondition on the act; the latent condition crystallizes from the
lived failure). Until earned, told-preconditions are legal as telling
(told-then-validated, rivals attach at first evidence).

**Known trap, and the owner's own cure:** pure overlap-greed is
hill-climbing — it can stall in a local maximum (a connectivity
desert between the frontiers). The random seeds from the first
design ARE the escape hatch; the diversity ruling applies — the beam
keeps a few LOW-overlap candidates on purpose. Court both beams
(pure-greedy vs seeded-diverse) and measure which bridges more.

Court metrics for S0, sharpened by this refinement: fraction of net
touched vs exhaustive; rings-to-bridge; bridge quality (route length /
conditions satisfied); greedy-vs-diverse beam win rate; refusal
honesty when no bridge exists (planted unbridgeable pairs).
