## The thesis: SCALE IS ANOTHER FORM OF SHAPE (owner)

The crystallizer vision already says token = group, holonic all the
way down. An ORDER is a group of rows. A DAY is a group of orders.
A CUSTOMER is a group of orders another way. "Look at orders" is
not a special organ — it is walking one level up a holarchy that
the substrate already believes in. The concept tower proved rung 1:
ONE repetition-detector found the same structure in numbers, words,
and space — structure detached from its material. The lens loop is
the same move for METHODS: one small operator vocabulary, composed,
courted, minted — so a new way of looking is discovered the way a
new word was grown (the growing grammar, but for lenses).

## Why the engine already holds the pieces

1. **Methods are nodes** (the keystone): a lens can be an act: node
   with a slot signature, like everything else he owns.
2. **A-to-X seek already composes routes from signatures** — he
   re-derived his own six-stage pipeline by backward chaining,
   routes never seeded. Composing re-aggregate ∘ split-sign ∘
   concentrate is the same machinery pointed at data.
3. **discover_harder is the genesis loop**: scream → aim → compose
   → court → mint. The scream here is RESIDUE: an account that
   refuses, a law that will not certify, negatives unexplained.
4. **The courts already refuse nonsense** — which is the honest way
   he LEARNS measure-vs-label: summing invoice numbers yields laws
   that never certify. The refusal IS the lesson. Engine-side, the
   token lists I hand-coded (id/zip/price words) are INFORMATION
   and must not be installed; the structural fingerprints
   (cardinality ratio, sign mix, integer-ness, repetition — tower
   detectors) are SENSES, and senses are legal capability.

## The rung ladder (research-first; courts before promotion)

- **L0 THE FINGERPRINT SENSE**: structural probes as percepts —
  every column/table gets a shape-fingerprint (cardinality, sign
  mix, grain, repetition, monotony, absence pattern). Court: on
  the two real blind datasets, do fingerprints alone separate
  measure/identifier/coordinate/rate — NO name-token lists?
- **L1 LENS PRIMITIVES AS NODES**: re-aggregate(by), split(sign),
  complement(frame), cluster(time), derive(product/ratio),
  subsample-court(grade). Battery: given demo3 and the goal
  "account for the negative revenue," seek() must COMPOSE
  group-by-day ∘ split-sign ∘ concentrate and surface the planted
  Dec-7-style event — route never seeded.
- **L2 THE SCREAM WIRING**: residues auto-file goals (the frontier
  habit): an unexplained account becomes his own next question —
  self-directed looking, no human prompt.
- **L3 MINTING**: a lens-chain that survives its court becomes a
  named flow: node in his vocabulary — supersedable, receipted.
  His ways-of-looking GROW, which is the self-discovery the owner
  is asking for.
