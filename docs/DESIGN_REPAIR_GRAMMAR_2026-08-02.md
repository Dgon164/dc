## 2. The theoretical resolution process (the reference)

THE REPAIR LOOP, run whenever a failure fires:

  fire -> signature -> verb court -> repair -> outcome court -> file

1. FIRE: the failure enters the net as a node doored to the co-active
   context (law, batch, organs involved). No evaporating shrugs.
2. SIGNATURE: compute the four strengths with existing organs (the campaign
   proved all four computable): localized (cross_check pass-fraction with
   unique repairs), multiplicative (residual-discover same-form r2), bimodal
   (split-side UNDERSTOOD siblings, min r2), form-shaped (overturn, or
   new-form working replacement).
3. THE VERB COURT: argmax over strengths, with the gates the data demanded:
   - THE WORKING-REPLACEMENT GATE: supersede claims strength only when the
     replacement is UNDERSTOOD (or an actual OVERTURN) — refute-without-
     successor is not supersede-shaped (this gate alone took the court from
     3/4 to 4/4).
   - THE CLEAN-FLOOR GATE: floors feeding any signature are computed on the
     passing side only — corruption must never inflate the floor that then
     excuses it (the batch-A warped-law wound).
   - Ties or all-zero -> not a repair case: route to the diagnosis menu
     (D1-D8), file the open failure as an ask.
4. REPAIR, by verb — each with its success court:
   - CORRECT: apply the unique cell repairs; keep the law. Court: confirm on
     the repaired batch returns high r2_new.
   - ADJUST: refit constants on the new evidence; SAME form; the old
     constants become the superseded lineage entry (never averaged, never
     overwritten silently). Court: retuned law beats stale law on fresh
     validation, form unchanged (VARIANT filing).
   - ADD: split by flags; discover each side; seat both as siblings under
     one lawform family; neither population inherits the other's constants.
     Court: fresh mixed data resolves per-side (each sibling confirms high
     on its own regime).
   - SUPERSEDE: seat the replacement REFIT ON THE OVERTURNING EVIDENCE
     (never the rival's stale discovery-time constants); loser kept wearing
     its defeat; the readable equation meta rewritten in the same act.
     Court: replacement confirms high on fresh validation.
5. OUTCOME COURT: every repair's court result banks on the failure node
   (worked/failed). A failed repair keeps the failure OPEN and the attempt
   wrong-kept — returnable, minable.
6. FILE: standing hygiene laws the campaign earned — REFUTED = demotion
   (zombies outlawed); rivals never frozen (told laws receive rivals at
   first validation data); probes are drives (an unactioned probe is an open
   ask); the CONFLICT SCAN runs as proprioception-over-laws (predictions
   compared on shared domains; disagreement past floor fires F5 and files
   the duel himself); organs share (certificate informs adequacy).
